# StreamingTV for Roku (Beta)

**Free XC / M3U media player.** No charge, no accounts required to use the player itself.

**Built by Alfred Web Design**

---

## Why this exists

This app was built for a **private streaming service** (our client’s white-label product). To stay white-label and **follow Roku’s Channel Store rules**, we also ship it as a **generic XC / M3U player** — anyone can point it at their own playlist and watch.

That open player is what lets us be on the Roku store at all. We were **paid by our client** to build this. Making the player public is not a trick; it’s the clean way to list a player on Roku.

We are **not interested in your information**. We don’t sell data, we don’t want your playlists, and the source is here so you can check that nothing shady is going on.

---

## What it does

- **XC / M3U player** — enter your own server or playlist (or set up on our simple web page)
- **StreamingTV customers** — link the TV with a code from your Provider account
- **Playlists stay private** — credentials are encrypted on your device before upload; the server only stores a locked box it cannot open

**This app does not include any media content.** You bring your own playlist or service.

---

## Setup (XC / M3U)

1. Open the app on your Roku  
2. Scan the QR (or open the website shown on screen)  
3. Enter the **device ID** and **PIN** from the TV  
4. Add your XC server or M3U URL  
5. The TV picks it up automatically  

To change playlists later, hold the **`*`** button on the remote for a new code.

StreamingTV customers: choose **StreamingTV login** and enter the link code from your account page (or ask your Provider).
